Wake Me Up turns a morning alarm into a short route from waking to doing something for your day. Choose up to five steps: a focus task, comfortable movement, and a destination or small real-world action.

BUILD YOUR MORNING ROUTE
Thirteen challenges include push-ups, squats, dance, marching, stretch, breathing, sun taps, typed maths, memory sequences, a typed intention, a barcode or QR destination, letting in light, and preparing a drink. Reorder steps, save reusable routes, or start with a gentle, focus, or joyful preset. Camera push-up and phone motion estimates are optional. Guided counting and comfortable alternatives remain available.

MORE FLEXIBLE WAKE-UPS
Choose weekday repeats, a specific date, or a work/rest cycle. Skip the next occurrence without turning off the series, or change only its time. Add days off, pause for time away, or set a separate quick nap. Android system alarms work while the app is closed.

Android schedules the next occurrence and renews it when an alarm fires. Boot and timezone changes restore schedules. Keep the device powered on; force-stopping the app prevents alarms until you reopen it. Allow notifications and full-screen alarm display, and test the actual device before relying on an alarm.

STAY WITH YOUR MORNING
Enable an optional native wake-up check after completing a route. Confirm three sun taps or cancel it anytime. Choose system snooze duration, or turn snooze off and open the movement action. Android always retains system dismissal controls; the app cannot force exercise or guarantee that you wake.

SOUNDS AND REST
Six presets draw from three original instrumental tracks. Try changing sounds between mornings, a bundled gentle sound ramp, or local non-DRM audio import. Ritual music has its own volume and mute controls. An optional bedside clock, wind-down reminder, user-chosen rest goal, and app icon shortcuts make daily setup easier. Test sounds on your actual device before relying on them.

YOUR OWN HISTORY
Completed alarm routes add real sunrise entries, step details, optional mood, elapsed route time, and wake-up check confirmation. Practice earns no journal credit. See your last seven days, hide the consecutive-day streak, or export a CSV you choose where to share.

FREE AND PLUS
Free includes two saved alarms, one quick nap, every standard challenge, routes, core scheduling, wake-up checks, sounds, local journal, and standard practice. Plus offers up to twenty saved alarms, adjustable comfortable targets, and longer practice. Google Play displays regional monthly and yearly prices; restore purchases in Settings.

No app login, advertising, analytics SDKs, or developer backend. Camera frames are processed on device and never recorded or uploaded. Destination codes use a saved one-way fingerprint. For Android 10 or later, on phones and tablets.
