WAKE ME UP · EFFECTIVE 5 OCTOBER 2026

Wake Me Up is developed by Oana Rinaldi. It is a native iPhone and iPad alarm and movement-habit app. You can use it without an app account. We do not operate a backend for Wake Me Up, sell personal data, use advertising or analytics SDKs, or track you across other apps and websites.

Data on your device

Alarm times, repeat days, names, movement choices, targets, sound preferences, completed rituals, optional mood check-ins, and an unfinished ritual identifier are stored locally. The app uses Apple's AlarmKit to register your alarms with the operating system. Your device or iCloud backup may include local app data under your Apple settings. The developer does not receive these backups.

Optional camera and motion access

If you select camera counting, the front camera and Apple's Vision framework estimate push-up repetitions on your device. Camera frames are processed temporarily in memory. They are not recorded, saved, sent to the developer, or uploaded. Motion counting reads device acceleration temporarily to estimate active dancing time. Motion readings are not stored or uploaded. These permissions are optional: guided counting, timed dance, and gentle stretch work without camera or motion access.

Revoke access in device Settings → Apps → Wake Me Up. Camera and motion capture stop when you pause, leave the ritual, or the app enters the background. Counts are estimates, not biometric identity, medical diagnoses, or form assessments.

Apple subscriptions

Optional Plus purchases use Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes your payment and maintains your purchase history. The app checks verified transaction identifiers and subscription status to unlock Plus. We do not receive card details or operate our own purchase-account database. Apple's privacy terms govern its purchase processing.

Music, links, and support

The app's original soundtrack is bundled and plays locally. It does not send your data to Suno, ChatGPT, or another generation service. External privacy, support, and terms links open oanarinaldi.com, where ordinary website hosting logs may include connection information such as IP address and browser metadata. Apple subscription-management links use Apple's services. Contacting support by email shares the information you choose to include with the developer and your email provider; avoid sending private camera images, payment details, or passwords.

Your choices and deletion

Settings → Clear sunrise journal removes completed ritual entries and the local streak. Delete alarms in each alarm editor to cancel them with the system. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to any device backups you control. You can manage backups in Apple Settings. Deleting the app or journal does not cancel a subscription; cancel through Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Children and changes

Wake Me Up is a general-audience habit tool and does not knowingly collect children's data on developer servers. Exercise must be comfortable and appropriate for the person using it. We will update this policy if the app's data practices change.
