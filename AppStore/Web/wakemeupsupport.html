Support — Wake Me Up

WAKE ME UP · IPHONE & IPAD

Set an alarm and build a morning route

Open Morning → Set my first alarm. Choose a time, schedule, sound, and first move. Build a route of up to five ordered steps, or use a ready-made gentle, focus, or joyful route. Each step can be edited or moved earlier or later. Save a route as a reusable preset. All thirteen standard challenges and multi-step routes are free; Plus unlocks adjustable targets and more saved alarms.

Thirteen challenges

Choose push-ups, squats, dance, marching, stretch, breathing, sun taps, typed maths, memory sequences, a typed intention, a destination barcode or QR check, letting in light, or preparing a drink. Guided movement uses an active timer or your own count. Light and drink actions are self-reported; the app does not measure them. Maths requires a correct typed answer. Memory lets you replay a short trail. Typed intentions ignore punctuation and letter case.

Destination checks

Save a barcode or QR code in Settings → Saved destination codes. Give it a useful name and choose it in a destination route step. Camera scanning matches the saved one-way fingerprint entirely on device. A guided check-in is always available when scanning is not comfortable or accessible. No camera footage is saved or uploaded.

Native alarms, snooze, and wake-up checks

Allow Alarms when saving an enabled alarm. Native AlarmKit works on iOS and iPadOS 26.1 or later. With snooze off, Start moving opens the route. With snooze on, the system Snooze button waits your chosen 3, 5, 10, or 15 minutes, and Stop opens the route. iOS retains its own dismissal controls. The app cannot force exercise, prevent power-off, or guarantee waking.

An optional still-awake check schedules another native alarm 3, 5, 10, or 15 minutes after a completed route. Confirm three sun taps and Yes, I\'m awake. Confirmation is recorded against the existing sunrise, without creating a second journal entry. You can cancel a pending check from Morning. It does not punish skipping or repeatedly re-ring.

Flexible schedules and the calendar horizon

Use weekday repeats, a specific date, or a repeating work/rest cycle. Skip next preserves your repeating alarm. Add specific days off in the editor, or pause enabled alarms until a chosen date in Morning tools. Ordinary weekday repeats continue until disabled. Work/rest cycles, exceptions, pauses, next-occurrence overrides, and rotating sounds use fourteen pre-scheduled native alarm dates. They refill when you open the app or interact with the alarm. The scheduled-through date is shown on Morning and Alarm readiness; dates beyond that horizon are not yet scheduled. Open the app regularly and refresh the plan before the horizon ends.

Quick naps and bedside clock

Morning tools lets you set one quick nap from 10 to 90 minutes in addition to your saved alarms. A new nap replaces the previous nap. The optional bedside clock has a dim appearance inside the app and shows your next alarm. Native alarms do not need the bedside clock open. Keep the device powered on.

Sound choices and imports

Six presets draw from three original instrumental Suno tracks: First Light, Soft Start, Rise & Shine, Bright Marimba, Quiet Keys, and Morning Groove. Choose a different sound each morning, or import a non-DRM audio file you have permission to use. Imports must be smaller than 50 MB, mono or stereo, and 96 kHz or below. The first 25 seconds are saved locally as an alarm clip. Preview your chosen sound. Bundled gentle variants ramp up over 15 seconds; imported clips play as provided. iOS controls system alarm volume. The separate ritual music slider only changes music inside the route.

Camera and motion counting

Camera push-up estimates work best with a secured device at floor level, facing your side, with shoulder, elbow, wrist, and hip visible. Low light and camera angle affect counting. Use guided counting for modified push-ups or unreliable detection. Dance and marching can use optional motion sensing with a securely held phone. On iPad, guided mode is more comfortable. All permissions are optional. Stop if a movement is uncomfortable.

Journal, route recovery, and export

Complete every route step and save your sunrise. The app checkpoints completed steps so a reopened route can resume from its current step. An interrupted timed step resumes from its start. The journal records step types, completion modes, mood, elapsed route time including pauses, and optional wake-up confirmation. Practice never adds journal credit. Multiple alarms on one day count as one bright morning for the streak. Hide the streak in Settings if you prefer a gentler view.

The last-seven-days view uses your actual completed routes, not sleep tracking. Export a CSV from Settings or Sunrises and choose its destination in the system share sheet. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Wind-down reminders and shortcuts

Choose your own planned rest duration for a suggested wind-down time. This is planning, not sleep measurement or medical advice. An optional bedtime reminder asks for notification permission. Siri and Shortcuts can set a quick nap, start practice, or tell you your next enabled wake-up. No app account is needed.

Test before relying on an alarm

Open Alarm readiness and schedule the one-minute sound test. It uses the quick-nap slot. Lock the actual device and listen, including with Silent mode and Focus. Check alarm permission, system volume, and custom sound playback. Simulator tests cannot verify the sound on your physical device. Refresh special calendar plans before their shown horizon ends. Alarms do not sync between devices.

Free and Plus

Free includes two saved alarms, one quick nap, all thirteen challenges, multi-step routes, standard targets, flexible schedules, wake-up checks, six presets, sound import, journal, export, and unlimited standard practice. Plus provides up to twenty saved alarms, custom comfortable targets, and longer practice. Monthly and yearly plans unlock the same Plus features. Apple supplies your regional prices. No app login or ads are required.

Restore, cancel, and contact

Restore purchases in Settings with the Apple Account used to subscribe. Manage or cancel through Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. Deleting the app does not cancel billing.

Email oanaunciuleanu@gmail.com with your device model, iOS version, and issue. Do not include passwords, payment details, or private camera footage.
