WAKE ME UP · IPHONE & IPAD

Set your first alarm

Open Morning → Set my first alarm. Choose a time, repeat days, movement, and sound. Turn Enable this alarm on, then Save. Allow Apple's alarm permission when asked. The app uses native AlarmKit on iOS and iPadOS 26.1 or later. Alarms are local to the device; they do not sync between iPhone and iPad.

How the movement ritual works

When your alarm rings, open its Start moving action. The system alarm stops and your ritual opens. iOS always retains its own alarm dismissal controls, so the app cannot force you to exercise or prevent a system alarm from being stopped. The app does not automatically re-ring to punish skipping. You can exit safely without saving a sunrise.

Alarm troubleshooting

Test an alarm on your own device before relying on it. Check Settings → Apps → Wake Me Up → Alarms, device volume, and the app's enabled toggle. Keep your device powered on. Restart after changing permissions if necessary. A switched-off device cannot ring. Saved alarms display local time and repeat on the selected weekdays; daylight-saving changes follow the system calendar.

Push-ups, dance, and gentle stretch

Camera push-up counting is optional and works best with the device secured at floor level, facing your side, with your shoulder, elbow, wrist, and hip visible. Low light, occlusion, and camera angle may affect counting. It estimates a full straight-arm → lowered → straight-arm cycle. Use guided counting for wall push-ups, knee modifications, or unreliable detection. Tap One push-up done after each repetition.

Dance in guided mode for an active timer, or use motion mode with a phone held securely or in a pocket. Motion mode counts only detected movement, which can be imperfect. On iPad, guided mode is more comfortable. Gentle stretch uses a timed seated or standing routine. Stop if a movement is uncomfortable; no camera or motion permission is required for guided routines.

More ways to wake up

Easy squats use an honest manual repetition count. Morning march uses a guided timer or optional motion sensing. Slow breaths provides a four-second inhale and six-second exhale guide without measuring your breathing. Follow the sun asks you to tap the highlighted sun as it moves between four spots, for a seated or in-bed challenge. All seven standard challenges are free.

Choose a ringtone

In the alarm editor, choose First Light (marimba), Soft Start (gentle piano), or Rise & Shine (upbeat funk), and use Preview selected sound. A 25-second version plays through the system alarm; matching original music accompanies the ritual if enabled in Settings.

Your sunrise journal

Complete an alarm ritual and select Save my sunrise. A streak counts consecutive days with completed alarm rituals. More than one alarm on the same day still counts as one bright morning. Practice never adds streak credit. If you leave the app mid-ritual, return to continue from the start without automatic completion.

Free and Plus

Free includes two alarms, all seven challenges, camera and motion counting, original sounds, journal, and unlimited standard practice. Plus provides up to 20 alarms, adjustable movement targets, and a longer dance practice session. Monthly and yearly plans unlock the same Plus features. Prices shown in the app come from Apple for your region. Existing saved alarms are retained when Plus expires; adding extra alarms and changing premium targets requires an active subscription.

Restore and cancel

Use Settings → Restore purchases with the Apple Account used to subscribe. Manage or cancel with Settings → Manage subscriptions or Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Apple handles billing and refund requests through reportaproblem.apple.com. No app login is required. Deleting the app does not cancel billing.
